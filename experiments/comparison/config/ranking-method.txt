Fit one linear pairwise-logistic score per target family. Native-bin-resolved training pairs supply labels. Three authority-grouped inner folds select penalties 1e-4 through 1 by pair errors, scoring ties as half an error and choosing stronger regularisation on equal losses. Refit feature transformations inside each fold; calibration labels are unused. The design uses development data; it is not independent confirmation or a loss-only causal comparison. Stable scalar reductions, objective/gradient checks and nonzero failure outcomes are retained.
